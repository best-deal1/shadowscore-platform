# ShadowScore V16 - Marketplace & Payment Risk Intelligence

## What changed

This version sharpens the product focus.

ShadowScore is no longer positioned as a generic URL or scam checker.  
The current product focus is:

**Marketplace & Payment Risk Intelligence**

## Core message

**Know Your Risk Before They Do™**

ShadowScore helps sellers identify marketplace, verification, compliance and payout risks before enforcement actions affect revenue, account access or payment flow.

## Key updates

- Removed URL Intelligence from the front-page positioning.
- Added Case Intelligence Engine section.
- Added clearer Risk Timeline.
- Added Restriction Probability.
- Added Confidence Score visibility from the risk engine.
- Added structured Case Library.
- Added new risk rules:
  - eBay MC081 registration suspension
  - Amazon retail sourcing / resale detection
  - TikTok Shop fair trading enforcement
  - Product quality / low rating / deferred settlement risk
- Strengthened Payment Health positioning:
  - PayPal
  - Payoneer
  - Stripe
- Improved homepage focus around marketplace evidence, not rumors or generic URL claims.

## Important product boundary

ShadowScore does not analyze gossip, public campaigns or unverified allegations.  
The engine is designed around seller-supplied evidence such as:

- Marketplace notices
- Seller dashboards
- Account health screenshots
- Payout messages
- Verification requests
- Tracking records
- Supplier documents
- Payment processor messages
